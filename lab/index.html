    Model, formulas and things to try

    What is simulated

    • A serial line of N stations. Station k has mk machines, a process time STk, a process batch bk (parts processed together), a move lot (parts moved together), an OEE (times are divided by it) and, in part 2, an automatic cycle that the machine runs without the worker.
    • Buffers have a capacity: a finished part that finds the next buffer full stays on its machine, which is blocked.
    • WIP w counts every job in the system, including the queue before S1; LT goes from entry to exit, so WIP = TR · LT (Little).
    • In part 2 every manual operation needs a worker; a worker with speed v does ST in ST/v.
    • Exact event-driven simulation: with deterministic times the results match the formulas to the last digit.

    Formulas

    • WIP = TR · LT Little's law
    • TRb = min m·b/t bottleneck rate
    • W0 = TRb · T0 critical WIP
    • best: TR = min(w/T0, TRb)
    • worst: TR = 1/T0, LT = w·T0
    • PWC: TR = w/(W0+w−1)·TRb
    • TR ≤ min(n/VAT, TRb) labor or machines
    • LTwip = (w/n)·VAT tied workers, w ≥ n

    Things to try in class

    • Penny Fab: raise w from 1 to 8 and compare best case, worst case (move lot = w) and exponential times (PWC).
    • Bottleneck preset: enlarge the 1-place buffer before S3 and see the blocking of S2 disappear.
    • Pizza shop: from 2 to 4 workers, LT = 80 → 40 min (−50%).
    • Bucket brigade: order slow → fast, then fast → slow, and watch the space–time diagram.
    • One operator, three machines: add machines until the worker becomes the constraint.
